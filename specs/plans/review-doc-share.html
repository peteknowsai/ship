Review: Share a ship doc

What shipped, in plain terms. Already landed. Two things only you can check are at the bottom.

What you got

  • A Share button, bottom right, on every ship storyboard, plan card, review card and doc in your Chrome. Press it and the link is on your clipboard.
  • The link lives at share.peteknowsai.xyz on your PKAI account, unguessable and hidden from search. The apex stays free.
  • When ship redraws a page you shared, the same link shows the new round within seconds. The button says "Shared · updated just now".
  • Nick and Bonnie see the page exactly as you do, with a footer saying who shared it and when, and arrows back through every earlier version.
  • Stop sharing in the Shared panel takes the page and all its versions down.

Flagged

  • Two uploads of the same page at the same instant can overwrite one version. Needs a redraw and a Share press within a second; left as is.
  • The packer skips srcset images and CSS @import. Ship docs don't use them.
  • A doc you open by hand whose file name differs from its ship's name gets its own link that redraws won't follow. Ship names files by slug, so it doesn't happen today.

Reports, not work: say fix now, backlog or ignore.
