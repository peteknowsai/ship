Review: gate buttons

ship, gate buttons (PR #238). When a ship parks at a gate, you answer it from the prompt with one key.

What you got

  • Buttons above the prompt at every gate. Storyboard: 1 show storyboard, 2 lock it. Go: 1 show plan card, 2 go. Try it: 1 open the app.
  • A lone digit in an empty prompt presses it, after a short pause, so a typed reply like "1abc" stays a reply. Clicking works too.
  • "Show" brings that page's tab to the front of Chrome, the same tab ship already opened, not a second copy.
  • "Lock it" and "go" go in as your words, and the buttons hide until that turn ends, so a double press answers once.
  • "merge main" never gets a button.
