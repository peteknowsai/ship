What gets built

Press Share on any ship doc, paste the link to Nick or Bonnie, and it follows every redraw with the old rounds a click back.

  1. A ship-share site at share.peteknowsai.xyz on your PKAI Cloudflare account: it stores every version of a shared page, serves the newest at the plain link, and draws the footer with the arrows.
  2. A storage bucket and a secret key for it: the key lives in your Keychain and on the site, nowhere else.
  3. A small share script on your Mac that packs a page with its styles, images and frames into one file, uploads it, and copies the link to your clipboard.
  4. The bridge that lets the Ship tabs extension call that script, the same way your Tyler handoff works.
  5. The Share button, its Sharing, Link copied, Shared and Couldn't share states, and Stop sharing, drawn by the extension on every ship doc.
  6. show.sh re-uploads a doc you've shared each time ship redraws it, so the link follows the rounds.
  7. Tests for the packer, the site and the button, then a real share, redraw, flip back and stop, run end to end before it lands.
  8. Docs in the ship repo's CLAUDE.md, and a line in the pipeline skill so every ship knows redraws republish.
